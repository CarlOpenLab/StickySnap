核心循环,三步。The core loop, in three keystrokes.

不跳编辑器、不弹对话框——从想法到「留在桌面上」,只按三次键。No editor hop, no dialogs — from thought to "on the desktop" in three keystrokes.

F1

截图Capture

全屏冻结,光标下的窗口自动高亮。单击选中,或拖拽框选;放大镜取色,方向键微调。The screen freezes; the window under your cursor lights up. Click to grab it or drag a frame. Magnifier to pick a color, arrow keys to nudge.

Enter

贴上Pin

截图直接钉上桌面:置顶悬浮,滚轮缩放,Ctrl+滚轮调透明度,不挡工作流。The shot is pinned on top of your desktop — scroll to zoom, Ctrl+scroll for opacity. Present, never in the way.

F3

随取Retrieve

剪贴板里的任何图片,F3 一下变成桌面贴图;重启电脑,贴图自动恢复原位。Any image on your clipboard becomes a desktop pin, instantly. Reboot, and every pin returns exactly where you left it.

贴图和标注不是附加功能,是产品本身——每一处都按「天天用」打磨。Pins and annotations are not add-ons here — they are the product, polished for daily use.

它凭什么常驻托盘?Why it earns a spot in your tray.

M0 版本已经做到的,不是承诺——是现在就能按出来的手感。This is what M0 already does — not promises, but keystrokes you can press today.

  • 窗口智能吸附Snap to windows

    光标掠过,窗口自动高亮;自动跳过贴图、桌面和隐身窗口,单击即选中。The window under your cursor highlights as you move — pins, the desktop and hidden windows are skipped. One click selects it.

  • 像素级手感Pixel-precise feel

    9×9 放大镜、#RRGGBB 取色、实时尺寸提示、方向键微调——精确到像素。A 9×9 magnifier, #RRGGBB readout, live size label, arrow-key nudging — precise down to the pixel.

  • 一步式标注Annotate in place

    矩形、椭圆、箭头、画笔、荧光笔、马赛克;9 色 3 粗细,Ctrl+Z 随时反悔。Rectangle, ellipse, arrow, pen, highlighter, mosaic — 9 colors, 3 widths, and Ctrl+Z to take it all back.

  • 贴图常驻Pins persist

    置顶悬浮、滚轮缩放、鼠标穿透;会话落盘,重启电脑自动恢复原位。Always on top, scroll to zoom, click-through mode. The session is saved to disk — pins return after a reboot.

  • 多屏 HiDPIMulti-monitor, HiDPI

    全程物理像素:多显示器拼接、混合缩放、跨屏选区,坐标都准确。Physical pixels all the way down — correct across multi-monitor stitching, mixed scaling and cross-screen selections.

  • 纯本地运行Local-only

    无网络请求、无上传、无遥测;贴图也不会出现在下一次截图里。No network requests, no uploads, no telemetry — and pins never appear in your next screenshot.
