核心循环,三步。The core loop, in three keystrokes.

不跳编辑器、不弹对话框——从想法到「留在桌面上」,只按三次键。No editor hop, no dialogs — from thought to "on the desktop" in three keystrokes.

F1

截图Capture

全屏冻结,光标下的窗口自动高亮。单击选中,或拖拽框选;放大镜取色,方向键微调。The screen freezes; the window under your cursor lights up. Click to grab it or drag a frame. Magnifier to pick a color, arrow keys to nudge.

Enter

贴上Pin

截图直接钉上桌面:置顶悬浮,滚轮缩放,Ctrl+滚轮调透明度,不挡工作流。The shot is pinned on top of your desktop — scroll to zoom, Ctrl+scroll for opacity. Present, never in the way.

F3

随取Retrieve

剪贴板里的任何图片,F3 一下变成桌面贴图;重启电脑,贴图自动恢复原位。Any image on your clipboard becomes a desktop pin, instantly. Reboot, and every pin returns exactly where you left it.

  • 一步式标注Annotate in place

    无网络请求、无上传、无遥测;贴图也不会出现在下一次截图里。No network requests, no uploads, no telemetry — and pins never appear in your next screenshot.

贴图,不止「浮在上面」。Pins that actually stay put.

很多工具的贴图是临时浮窗:退出就没了。StickySnap 把「留得住、不添乱」当成底线:重启能恢复、不进截图、可以点穿。For most tools a pin is a temporary floating window — gone on exit. StickySnap treats persistence, capture exclusion and click-through as the baseline.

持久化贴图Pins persist

图片与位置落盘保存,重启电脑、资源管理器崩溃重来,贴图都回到原位。Image and position are written to disk, so every pin comes back exactly where you left it — even after a reboot.

贴图不进截图Pins stay out of shots

贴图窗口对截图接口隐藏(WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE):截图里只有你要的内容,不会多出一层参考图。Pins are hidden from capture APIs (WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE), so your next screenshot holds only what you meant to capture.

点穿Click-through

右键一键鼠标穿透,贴图变成只看不挡的参考层;托盘菜单还能一次「取消所有穿透」。One right-click makes a pin click-through — a reference layer that never blocks your work. Clear them all at once from the tray.
